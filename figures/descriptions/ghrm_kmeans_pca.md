# ghrm_kmeans_pca

**Purpose:** provides a two-dimensional PCA projection of the selected GHRM k=2 clustering.

**Cluster summary:** cluster 0 n=198, mean FEP 3.851; cluster 1 n=122, mean FEP 3.041.

**Interpretation:** visually summarizes separation found in the standardized five-construct space.

**Limitation:** clustering was not performed on the two displayed PCA axes; PCA is visualization only.
