# job_change_kmeans_criteria

**Purpose:** evaluates K-Means candidates k=2..7 on standardized numeric Job Change inputs.

**Selected solution:** k=3, SSE 11219.41, Silhouette 0.5773, Davies-Bouldin 0.6639.

**Interpretation:** this is the clearest internal clustering result among the four datasets.

**Limitation:** good geometric separation does not by itself establish meaningful HR causal segments.
