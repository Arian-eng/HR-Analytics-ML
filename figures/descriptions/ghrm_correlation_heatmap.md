# ghrm_correlation_heatmap

**Purpose:** shows construct-level Pearson correlations among GRS, GTD, GPA, GCM, GEE and FEP.

**Correlations with FEP:** GRS 0.646, GTD 0.645, GPA 0.611, GCM 0.646, GEE 0.590.

**Interpretation:** all five GHRM practice constructs are positively associated with FEP in this sample.

**Limitation:** correlation is observational and must not be described as causation.
