# chart9_f1_comparison_across_algorithms

**Purpose:** compares held-out F1 across Decision Tree, Random Forest, LinearSVC and MLP for the three classification benchmarks.

**Best F1 in committed run:** IBM MLP 0.5000; Job Change RF 0.6042; Promotion MLP 0.5054.

**Interpretation:** focuses comparison on minority-class performance rather than accuracy alone.

**Source:** committed classification JSON reports.

**Limitation:** rankings are specific to the documented split, tuning grid and preprocessing.
