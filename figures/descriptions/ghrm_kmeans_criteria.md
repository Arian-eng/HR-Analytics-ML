# ghrm_kmeans_criteria

**Purpose:** reports SSE, Silhouette and Davies-Bouldin across k=2..7 for standardized GHRM practice scores.

**Selected solution:** k=2, SSE 770.65, Silhouette 0.4363, Davies-Bouldin 0.8611.

**Interpretation:** the two-cluster solution has the strongest Silhouette under the documented selection rule.

**Limitation:** cluster selection is an internal validity criterion and does not prove naturally discrete employee types.
